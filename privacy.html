Privacy Policy - Ruam Tad Tor

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Ruam Tad Tor ("the app") is a private desktop application that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used by its owner to review the performance of the owner's own YouTube channels.

1. Data the app accesses

Only after the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app requests two read-only scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: channel name, the channel's videos, titles, publish dates, view, like and comment counts.
  • yt-analytics.readonly: YouTube Analytics reports for the owner's channel (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources, subscribers gained/lost, and aggregated anonymous audience statistics such as age group, country and device).

The app does not access Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data, and never uploads, modifies or deletes anything on YouTube.

2. How the data is used

Only to show the owner reports about their own videos on their own computer, to alert the owner when a video performs below the channel's usual level, and to help the owner plan better videos.

3. Storage, sharing and security

All data and Google tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no server and no account system. The data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising and never used to train AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the owner's computer until the owner deletes it. The owner can disconnect at any time with the app's Disconnect button (which revokes the token at Google) or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

5. YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
